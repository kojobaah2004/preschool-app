# Project Scope: Preschool Learning App

## In the MVP
- Alphabet lesson
- Numbers lesson
- Colours and shapes
- Quiz (picture-based)
- Stars and badges
- Progress tracking
- 4 roles: login and basic dashboards (Child, Teacher, Parent, Admin)

## Future Work
- Stories
- Videos
- Songs and rhymes
- Full games library
- Local-language vocabulary (a few words if time allows)
- Advanced analytics

## Rule
Scope is frozen. Anything new goes under Future Work, not the MVP.
